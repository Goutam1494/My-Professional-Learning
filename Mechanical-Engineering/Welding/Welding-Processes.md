
## Welding Processes

1. Introduction
Welding is a joining process used to
join two or more metal components.

## 2. Types of Welding

1. SMAW (Arc Welding)
2. GMAW (MIG Welding)
3. GTAW (TIG Welding)
4. FCAW
5. SAW (Submerged Arc Welding)
6. Gas Welding

## 3. Industrial Applications

- Structural fabrication
- Pipeline welding
- Equipment repair
- Steel plant maintenance
- Tank fabrication

## 4. Welding Safety

- Wear welding helmet.
- Use safety gloves.
- Wear protective clothing.
- Ensure proper ventilation.
- Check earthing connections.
- Keep fire extinguishers nearby.

## 5. Learning Objective

Understand common welding processes
and their industrial applications.
