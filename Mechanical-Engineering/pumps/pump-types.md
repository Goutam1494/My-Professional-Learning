
# Industrial Pumps

## 1. Introduction
Pumps are mechanical devices used to
transfer liquids from one location to another.

## 2. Types of Pumps

1. Centrifugal Pump
2. Gear Pump
3. Screw Pump
4. Reciprocating Pump
5. Diaphragm Pump
6. Submersible Pump

## 3. Industrial Applications

- Cooling water systems
- Boiler feed water
- Hydraulic systems
- Process water transfer
- Wastewater treatment

## 4. Maintenance Checklist

- Check vibration.
- Check bearing temperature.
- Inspect mechanical seals.
- Check lubrication.
- Inspect leakage.
- Check suction and discharge pressure.

## 5. Learning Objective
Understand industrial pump types,
applications and maintenance.
