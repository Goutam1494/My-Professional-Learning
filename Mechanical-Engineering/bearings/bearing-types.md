
Industrial Bearings

1. Introduction
Bearings support rotating shafts and
reduce friction between moving parts.

2. Types of Bearings

1. Deep Groove Ball Bearing
2. Angular Contact Ball Bearing
3. Cylindrical Roller Bearing
4. Spherical Roller Bearing
5. Tapered Roller Bearing
6. Thrust Ball Bearing
7. Needle Roller Bearing

3. Industrial Applications

- Conveyor systems
- Rolling mills
- Electric motors
- Pumps
- Gearboxes
- Fans and blowers

4. Maintenance Points

- Check lubrication.
- Monitor temperature.
- Check vibration.
- Inspect abnormal noise.
- Check alignment.
- Inspect bearing damage.

5. Learning Objective

Understand bearing types, applications
and basic maintenance practices.
